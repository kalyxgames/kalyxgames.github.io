Break of Dawn

A hex-grid tower defense deckbuilder where you choose which enemies join the army marching on you, and every tower rank is paid for in enemies killed or taken alive.

You lead a cult holding a portal open against the New Dawn. Every card places a building, and a full run fights through ten acts to a siege of the Keep.

The free demo is out now on Steam.
